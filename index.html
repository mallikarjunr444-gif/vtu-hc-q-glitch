Small-Sample Scarcity ($N \ll D$)

Rare-disease cohorts present severe class imbalance ($< 15\%$). While classical ensembles easily partition synthetic dense clusters, quantum kernels maintain structural geometry by projecting into exponential Hilbert dimensions.

Barren Plateau Mitigation

Variational Quantum Classifiers (VQC) with deep ansatz layers suffer from vanishing gradients. Q-Glitch limits ansatz depth to reps=3 and utilizes gradient-free COBYLA updates to ensure stable convergence.

Hardware Noise & Readout Error Mitigation

Under realistic NISQ hardware noise (depolarizing + readout error), Q-Glitch implements regularized readout error matrix inversion (TREX), achieving a 75.8% reduction in total variation error distance.

Hybrid Ensembling Supremacy

Neither pure classical nor pure quantum is optimal alone. Q-Glitch's weighted consensus ($0.40 \text{ RF} + 0.35 \text{ QSVC} + 0.25 \text{ VQC}$) leverages classical speed for routine cases and quantum state fidelity for boundary arbitration.
